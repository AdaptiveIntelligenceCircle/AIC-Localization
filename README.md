# AIC-Localization

**Language packs and translation process for Adaptive Intelligence Circle orientation materials.**

Status: **pre-Covenant**, experimental, under-claim.  
**DPGA context:** AIC has submitted materials related to the Digital Public Goods Alliance review process (under review as of November 2026). This localization repository is a supporting effort and is not itself a separately certified Digital Public Good.

This repository holds translations and localization aids for selected AIC public documents.

It does **not** create new protocol rules, does not alter technical specifications, and does not constitute official legal interpretation in any language.

---

## Purpose

- Make key orientation materials accessible in languages beyond English, starting with Vietnamese.
- Keep a clear record of what has been translated and at what review level.
- Provide a shared glossary so that core terms (Third Path, Ethical Kernel, fail-closed, NeedHuman, entity ≠ immunity, …) stay consistent.
- Support Global South and multilingual contributors without diluting under-claim discipline.
- Reduce barriers to understanding while preserving the original caution and limitations of the source texts.

---

## Canonical language

**English is the canonical language** for technical precision, formal models, and any text that could be read as normative.

1. Prefer the English source for technical and policy meaning.

---

## Do No Harm & Non-Capture (Localization-specific)

Localization carries specific risks. Therefore this repository observes the following additional constraints:

- Translations must not strengthen claims beyond the English source.
- Translations must not soften or remove under-claim, fail-closed, or non-capture language.
- No translation may create the appearance that AIC has special legal status, immunity, or authority in any jurisdiction.
- No translation may introduce token-related rule-power, fundraising claims, or promises of readiness that the source does not contain.
- When a term is ambiguous, prefer the more cautious rendering and document the uncertainty.
- Translators and reviewers share responsibility for preventing claim inflation.

These rules are not optional. They are part of the integrity of the work.
