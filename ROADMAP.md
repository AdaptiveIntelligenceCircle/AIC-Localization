---

## Explicit non-goals

- Rapid expansion of languages
- Treating translations as independent normative sources
- Using localization as a growth or marketing channel
- Claiming that multilingual availability equals DPG readiness or impact

---

## Review principle for every contribution

Before accepting a translation or change, ask:

1. Does this preserve or weaken the original under-claim posture?
2. Does this introduce any appearance of special status, readiness, or authority?
3. Is the more cautious rendering preferred when meaning is uncertain?

If the answer to question 1 or 2 is unfavorable, the contribution should be revised or declined.

---

*Localization is a responsibility, not a race.  
Accuracy and restraint come before coverage.*
