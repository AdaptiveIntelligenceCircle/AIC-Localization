# DPG Context and Accessibility

**Status:** Draft – November 2026  
**Tone:** Under-claim

---

## 1. Purpose of this note

This note records how AIC-Localization relates to discussions around Digital Public Goods (DPGs) and accessibility, in light of AIC’s submission to the Digital Public Goods Alliance review process.

It is intentionally limited in scope.

---

## 2. What localization can contribute

High-quality localization can:

- Lower language barriers for contributors and readers in non-English-speaking regions, including parts of the Global South.
- Help maintain consistent understanding of core concepts (Third Path, Ethical-from-kernel, fail-closed, NeedHuman, etc.).
- Support the principle that important orientation materials should not be available only to English-proficient audiences.

These outcomes are compatible with the broader aspiration that useful digital materials should be accessible and inspectable.

---

## 3. What localization does not claim

- Localization does not make AIC a certified Digital Public Good.
- Localization does not create new rights, status, or authority in any language.
- Localization does not guarantee that translated materials will be correctly understood or correctly applied.
- Localization does not remove or weaken the limitations, disclaimers, and under-claim posture of the original English texts.

---

## 4. Risk specific to translation

Translation can accidentally inflate claims.  

Examples of prohibited drift:

- Turning “experimental / pre-Covenant” into language that sounds ready or authoritative.
- Softening “not legal advice” or “entity ≠ immunity”.
- Adding implications of endorsement, partnership, or readiness that the source does not contain.

The review process for translations exists primarily to prevent this class of error.

---

## 5. Summary position

AIC-Localization is a supporting effort focused on careful accessibility.  

It aims to remain consistent with values of openness and reduced barriers, while strictly preserving the caution, limitations, and non-capture posture of the Adaptive Intelligence Circle materials it translates.

Any stronger claims would violate the purpose of this repository.
