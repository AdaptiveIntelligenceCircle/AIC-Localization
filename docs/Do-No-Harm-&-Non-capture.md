# Do No Harm & Non-Capture in Localization

Localization is not neutral. Poorly handled translation can quietly change the meaning and strength of claims.

This repository therefore enforces the following rules:

1. **No claim inflation**  
   Translations must not make AIC appear more advanced, more authoritative, more legally protected, or more ready than the English source states.

2. **No softening of caution**  
   Words such as “experimental”, “pre-Covenant”, “under-claim”, “not legal advice”, “entity ≠ immunity”, and “fail-closed” must retain their force in every language.

3. **No creation of status**  
   No translation may suggest that AIC, its contributors, or its documents enjoy special legal immunity, official recognition, or privileged standing in any jurisdiction.

4. **No introduction of economic or political rule-power**  
   Translations must not add implications about tokens, funding power, political alignment, or guaranteed outcomes.

5. **Prefer the more cautious rendering**  
   When a term or sentence can be read in more than one way, choose the reading that is more limited and document the choice if needed.

6. **Shared responsibility**  
   Both translators and reviewers are accountable for preventing drift. Silence or convenience is not an acceptable reason to allow stronger language.

These constraints are extensions of the core AIC principles (Third Path, Ethical-from-kernel, Under-claim, Non-capture) into the domain of language.
