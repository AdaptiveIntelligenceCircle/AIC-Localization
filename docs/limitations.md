# Limitations

## 1. Translations are aids, not new sources of authority

A clear Vietnamese (or other) text helps readers.  
It does not replace the English canonical text when meanings conflict, and it does not create new protocol rules.

## 2. Not legal advice in any language

Orientation about law or regulation remains orientation.  
No translation turns this material into legal advice or an official interpretation.

## 3. Coverage is incomplete

Only selected documents are prioritized.  
Absence of a translation does not mean the topic is unimportant; it means capacity and process have not yet delivered a reviewed version.

## 4. Quality varies by level

`draft` texts may contain awkward phrasing.  
`reviewed` / `stable` reduce risk but still yield to English on technical conflict.

## 5. Entity ≠ immunity

Localization does not grant AIC, translators, or any party special legal status.

## 6. Pre-Covenant

Translations must not advance or imply Covenant declaration or mainnet readiness.

## 7. Living process

Glossaries and preferred renderings can improve.  
When they change, older translations may need alignment.
