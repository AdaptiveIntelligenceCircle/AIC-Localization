# Overview — AIC-Localization

## Why localization exists here

AIC materials are written primarily in English.  
Many potential contributors and readers, especially in the Global South and in Vietnam, work more comfortably in other languages.  
Localization reduces unnecessary barriers while keeping the **same** boundaries: under-claim, pre-Covenant, no token rule-power, entity ≠ immunity.

## What is localized first

Priority is given to short orientation texts:

- what AIC is and is not,
- beginner paths (Start-Here style),
- core principles in compact form,
- selected FAQ answers,
- glossary of stable terms.

Long technical specifications and formal models stay English-first unless capacity allows careful, reviewed translation.

## Authority and conflict

| Situation | Resolution |
|-----------|------------|
| Translation clearer for a reader | Use the translation for understanding |
| Conflict on technical or normative meaning | English source wins |
| Legal or regulatory nuance | “Not legal advice” in every language; seek qualified local counsel for real decisions |

## Quality levels (used in status)

- **draft** — initial translation, may contain rough edges
- **reviewed** — checked by at least one additional person for fidelity and under-claim
- **stable** — used as the default for that language for the given document; still subordinate to English on conflict

## Non-goals

- Creating jurisdiction-specific “official” legal versions
- Marketing localization that softens caution
- Automatic machine translation without human review for priority documents
