# AIC — Short principles (English reference)

Status: pre-Covenant. Under-claim. Not legal advice.

1. **Transparency** — Prefer inspectable artifacts; no secret dual stack as a design goal.
2. **Ethical-from-kernel** — Place ethical constraints near authorization; evaluate toward Allow / Deny / NeedHuman.
3. **Fail-closed** — When permission conditions are not met, deny or restrict by default.
4. **Human gates** — NeedHuman is a first-class outcome and should not be silently bypassed.
5. **No token rule-power** — Money or tokens do not buy protocol law.
6. **Third Path** — Refuse pure state capture *and* pure commercial rule-power over the protocol.
7. **Entity ≠ immunity** — Participation does not make any entity immune from law or criticism.
8. **Under-claim** — Public language stays below maximum hype; community may be thin while architecture is real.
9. **Pre-Covenant** — Main surface is not declared until explicit criteria are met.
10. **Long-term stability** — Prefer controlled procedure over uncontrolled scale.

These lines are orientation aids for translators and readers.They do not replace formal specifications, checklists, or qualified advice where those are required.
